Open **Settings** from your avatar menu. Click a project's row to see its settings in a panel on
the right; click its number of deployments to go to the project's deployments instead.

## Who can change what

Every project member is a **Member** or an **Admin**. The panel shows only what your role can
use; the demo account can look but not change anything.

| Tab | Member | Admin |
|---|---|---|
| Details (name, description, website) and Delete | | Change |
| Members | See | See, invite, change roles and remove |
| Defaults | See | Change |
| Notifications | Change your own | Change your own |

If a default needs changing and you are not an admin, ask one of the project's admins; the
Members tab shows who they are.

An admin changes someone's role by clicking it in the Members tab. Nobody changes their own role
there; ask another admin.

**New project** appears for anyone in an organisation. Every account belongs to General, so most
people can create one there. If you belong to more than one organisation, you choose which one
the project goes in.

## Defaults

Defaults are the settings a camera takes when it is next deployed in the project: the triggering
method, the AI model, photos per trigger and the time between them, the detection threshold and
the capture flash. Cameras pick up a change at their next deployment, not straight away. Each
change saves as soon as you make it.

## Notifications

Notifications are yours alone; nobody else in the project sees or changes them. Tick a channel
for each event you want to hear about. In-app notifications work now. Email starts once an email
provider is set up for the site, and mobile notifications are sent by the Wildlife Watcher app.
For species detections you can type part of a species name, for example "rat", to hear only
about matching detections.

## Your password

Change your password under **Account** on the same page. The emailed reset link is only for when
you cannot sign in.
